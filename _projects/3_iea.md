**Apr 2025–present** | Institute of Advanced Studies (IEA USP) – Cátedra Oscar Sala

Researcher in the **Governance of Artificial Intelligence Agents** project, investigating the technical and ethical foundations of AI autonomy.

## Research Focus

Member of the **Technological Architectures Group**, investigating:

- **Interpretability:** Understanding and explaining AI decision-making processes
- **Interoperability:** Ensuring AI systems work effectively across platforms and domains
- **Autonomous Planning:** Studying how AI agents plan and execute complex tasks
- **Computational Efficiency:** Optimizing AI performance while maintaining transparency

## Project Context

The project analyzes:
- Technical and ethical foundations of AI autonomy
- Social risks and governance mechanisms
- Focus on the **Global South** perspective on AI development and governance

## Interdisciplinary Approach

Collaborating with researchers from computer science, policy, ethics, and social sciences to develop holistic perspectives on AI governance.
