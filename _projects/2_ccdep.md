**2025–present** | Data Science Center for Public Statistics (CCDEP) & SEADE Foundation

Master's researcher focused on public policies and investment analysis in São Paulo state.

## Project: PIESP (Survey of Announced Investments in São Paulo)

Developed the "News dataset for investment assessment in São Paulo state regions" in collaboration with the SEADE Foundation. This research contributes to:

- Understanding investment trends across state regions
- Informing public policy decisions
- Supporting economic development strategies
- Providing data-driven insights for regional planning

## Research Outputs

- **Publication:** "News dataset for investment assessment in São Paulo state regions," Proceedings of the 7th Dataset Showcase Workshop (2025) – DOI: 10.5753/dsw.2025.247813
