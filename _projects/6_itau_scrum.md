Scrum Master and Team Lead focused on agile team coaching, process optimization, and project success.

## Key Responsibilities

### Scrum Master Role
- **Led agile ceremonies:** Daily standups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, retrospectives
- **Process Coaching:** Helped team adopt and improve agile practices
- **Impediment Removal:** Identified and resolved blockers to team productivity
- **Team Health:** Monitored team dynamics and addressed interpersonal issues

### Team Lead Role
- **Performance Monitoring:** Tracked key performance indicators (KPIs) and team metrics
- **Risk Management:** Identified project and team risks early
- **Stakeholder Communication:** Communicated team status to management
- **Development:** Mentored team members and supported their growth

- **Team Velocity:** Optimized team workflow and productivity
- **Quality Assurance:** Maintained code quality and testing standards
- **Continuous Improvement:** Drove retrospective outcomes into actionable improvements

- Kanban practices for workflow visualization
